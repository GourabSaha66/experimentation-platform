## 5. Observational causal methods

### LaLonde job training (real data)

Treated group: 185 men from the randomised programme. Comparison group: 15,992 men from a national survey (CPS). The randomised experiment gives the benchmark answer.

| Estimate | Value ($ of 1978 earnings) |
|---|---|
| Randomised experiment (benchmark) | 1,794 |
| Naive comparison with survey controls | -8,498 |
| Propensity score matching (this tool), 95% interval -405 to 3,150 | 1,722 |
| DoWhy matching | 1,732 |
| EconML doubly robust (overlap region, n=692), 95% interval -1,933 to 2,877 | 472 |

- Treated units matched: 185 of 185. Largest covariate imbalance: 2.43 before, 0.13 after matching.
- DoWhy placebo-treatment refutation: placebo effect -261.3, p = 0.38.

### Simulated confounded data (true effect 2.0, 40 runs)

- Naive difference averaged **3.62** (biased). Matching averaged **2.04** (RMSE 0.13).
- DoWhy matching averaged 2.01 and EconML doubly robust 1.99 over the first 8 runs.

### Difference-in-differences

- Simulated panel, true effect 3.0 (60 runs): mean estimate **3.01**, RMSE 0.21, clustered 95% interval covered the truth 91.7% of the time.
- No real effect: placebo p-value below 0.05 in 0.0% of runs.
- Pre-trend test flags a problem in 8.3% of runs with parallel trends and 100.0% of runs where the groups were drifting apart.
- California organ donations (real data): effect -0.0225 (-0.0349 to -0.0100); clustered p = 0.0010, but placebo p = 0.185 because only one state was treated.

### Synthetic control
